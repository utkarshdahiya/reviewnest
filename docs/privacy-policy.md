# ReviewNest Privacy Policy
 
**Last updated: August 23, 2026**
 
This Privacy Policy explains how ReviewNest ("ReviewNest," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when merchants install our app on the Shopify platform, and when shoppers interact with the review widget on a merchant's storefront.
 
## 1. Who This Policy Covers
 
- **Merchants**: store owners and staff who install and use ReviewNest through the Shopify App Store.
- **Shoppers**: customers who visit a merchant's store and submit or view product reviews through ReviewNest's storefront widget.
## 2. Information We Collect
 
### From Merchants
- Shopify store name, domain, and store ID
- Shopify account/staff email address
- App configuration settings (widget appearance, review moderation preferences, etc.)
- Billing information processed through Shopify's billing system (we do not directly store payment card details)
### From Shoppers
- Name and email address submitted with a review
- Review content, star rating, and optional photos uploaded with a review
- Order/product information needed to verify a review is tied to a purchase (where applicable)
- Basic technical data such as IP address and browser type, for spam and fraud prevention
## 3. How We Use Information
 
We use collected information to:
 
- Operate and display the review widget on the merchant's storefront
- Store and moderate submitted reviews and photos
- Provide merchants with a dashboard to manage reviews
- Communicate with merchants about their account or app updates
- Detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations
We do not sell shopper or merchant personal information.
 
## 4. How We Store and Share Information
 
- Review data (text and ratings) is stored in our production database.
- Photos submitted with reviews are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Cloudflare R2).
- We do not share personal information with third parties except:
  - Service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, storage, infrastructure), under confidentiality obligations
  - Shopify, as required for the app to function on their platform
  - When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or property
## 5. Data Retention
 
We retain review data for as long as the merchant's store uses ReviewNest, or as needed to comply with legal obligations. Merchants can request deletion of their store's data by uninstalling the app or contacting us directly.
 
## 6. Shopper Rights and GDPR Compliance
 
In compliance with Shopify's data protection requirements and applicable privacy laws (including GDPR), ReviewNest supports:
 
- **Right to access**: shoppers or merchants can request a copy of the personal data we hold.
- **Right to deletion**: shoppers or merchants can request deletion of personal data.
- **Shop data redaction**: when a merchant uninstalls ReviewNest, we redact associated store data within the timeframe required by Shopify's app requirements.
These rights are supported through Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks (`customers/data_request`, `customers/redact`, `shop/redact`), which ReviewNest has implemented.
 
To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.
 
## 7. Cookies and Tracking
 
The ReviewNest storefront widget may use minimal cookies or local storage to prevent duplicate review submissions and improve performance. We do not use this data for advertising or cross-site tracking.
 
## 8. Children's Privacy
 
ReviewNest is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
